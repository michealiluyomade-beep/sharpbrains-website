(03) A DAY AT SHARP BRAINS

One Day, Told by the Hour

Gates open at 7:00am and the last bus pulls out by 4:00pm. Drag the dot or scroll the cards; they move together.

7:00 AM4:00 PM
7:00 AM

Resumption

Gates open. Uniform and hygiene check, bags dropped, one more hug at the gate.

7:30 AM

Morning Assembly

National anthem, pledge, a short devotion, and the day's announcements.

8:00 AM

Periods 1 & 2

Core subjects while concentration is freshest: Maths, English, and the Sciences.

9:40 AM

Short Break

A quick snack and some fresh air before the next set of lessons.

10:00 AM

Periods 3 & 4

Continued classwork across the timetable's core and elective subjects.

12:00 PM

Lunch Break

A proper meal, then free play and catching up with friends in the yard.

12:40 PM

Periods 5 & 6

Afternoon lessons, practicals, and class exercises.

2:00 PM

Clubs & Activities

Sports, clubs, music, and other extracurricular activities.

2:45 PM

Closing Assembly

A recap of the day, homework noted down, dismissal by class level.

3:00 PM

Closing & Pick-up

Buses load up, gates open for pick-up, with aftercare available on request.
