A Curriculum Built for Every Stage

From Nursery through SSS3, every level has a clear academic focus aimed at building confident, exam-ready students.

Nursery (Ages [2]–[5])

Core literacy, numeracy, basic science, and creative arts, building toward the Common Entrance exam.
